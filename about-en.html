A little space to pause

MIKUJI NOTE is an independent digital experience inspired by Japanese omikuji. It is not operated by a shrine or temple. Its fortunes are original texts intended for entertainment and reflection.

How the content is made

The guide lists the sources consulted for omikuji categories and customs. The fortune texts and illustrations were created with the assistance of generative AI; they are not copies of actual shrine fortune slips. Cultural information has been checked against the sources cited in the guide. The fortunes do not predict the future or constitute professional advice.

Privacy and data on your device

Your music on/off preference is stored in this browser’s local storage. The current demo does not include a feature for saving fortunes or notes. Records saved by an earlier version may still remain in your browser; you can remove them through your browser’s site-data settings.

This page loads Google Fonts, so font requests may be sent to Google. The current demo contains no advertising code or first-party analytics. A hosting provider may process access logs. Before launch, the privacy policy should be completed to reflect the hosting, analytics and advertising services actually used.
